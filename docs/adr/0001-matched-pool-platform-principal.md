# Matched pool with platform as principal

HardStuck MVP is a matched-pool boosting product, not an open seller marketplace. Clients configure a boost; the platform assigns a verified Booster; HardStuck is the merchant facing the Client. Boosters are contractors paid by the platform.

**Considered:** open marketplace (client-picked sellers), hybrid marketplace+pool at launch.

**Why:** Competitors’ boosting UX is queue/assignment-shaped; marketplace listings add seller KYC, warranties, and dispute surface we do not need for Solo/Duo rank boosts. Platform-principal simplifies checkout, ToS, and the escrow ledger.

**Consequences:** No client-facing seller storefront in MVP. Accounts/coaching marketplaces are a later domain, not a bolt-on flag.
